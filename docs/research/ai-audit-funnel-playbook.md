# Playbook: Selling AI Services via a Free 15-Min Audit That Upsells

## 1. The Funnel Model: Free Audit → Paid Audit → Build → Retainer

Three to four tiers. Each stage produces the data that justifies the next — and each stage is a qualification filter, not just a sale.

```
[Free 15-min audit call]  →  [Paid extensive audit]  →  [One-time build]  →  [Monthly retainer]
   foot-in-the-door            $1.5k–$15k flat            $1.5k–$50k           $2k–$8k/mo
   reveals ONE problem         scopes the whole thing     fixes the quick win  keeps building
```

- **Free tier** = max top-of-funnel, weaker qualification. **Paid entry** = fewer leads, far higher close rate and commitment. Both are legitimate; the paid-entry play (Jonathan Stark's $1,295 Roadmap, Ottley's $10k+ audits) "filters out non-committed prospects" and "distinguishes those who are serious" ([jonathanstark.com/roadmap](https://jonathanstark.com/roadmap); [optimizewithsanwal.com](https://optimizewithsanwal.com/ai-automation-agency-pricing-2026-a-cfos-guide/)).
- The same audit can be priced **3 ways by relationship stage**: free for cold leads (open doors), $250–$1,500 standalone for warm prospects, or bundled into a monthly retainer for active clients — re-run every 30/60/90 days ([mywebaudit.com](https://www.mywebaudit.com/blog/ai-visibility-audit-walkthrough)).
- Build/retainer ladder (one consultancy's model, consistent with several vendor data points — not a surveyed industry standard): paid audit **$5k–$15k** → build **$10k–$85k+** → retainer **$2k–$8k/mo** ([optimizewithsanwal.com](https://optimizewithsanwal.com/ai-automation-agency-pricing-2026-a-cfos-guide/)).
- **~71% of project clients convert to a retainer within 6 months** once they see results ([aibusiness.vc](https://aibusiness.vc/solo/how-to-charge-ai-automation-services)).

**The mechanic that links every stage (Hormozi):** give away the WHAT and WHY (the diagnosis — that a gap exists, and what it's costing), sell the HOW (implementation). The free audit must be a *complete* solution to a *narrow* problem that *reveals a bigger one* only your paid offer fixes ([blog.vidtao.com](https://blog.vidtao.com/alex-hormozi-lead-magnet-framework)). Withholding the insight backfires — if you give "sucky fluff," they conclude you only have fluff and buy elsewhere.

## 2. How to Run the Free 15-Min Audit (Minute-by-Minute)

This is a **live call** (Zoom screen-share or in person), never an emailed PDF. Live delivery is the consistent conversion mechanism — it gives you real-time narrative control, objection handling, and a natural transition into the pitch ([mywebaudit.com](https://www.mywebaudit.com/blog/ai-visibility-audit-walkthrough)).

**Before the call (10 min prep):** Research the specific person and business — not the title. Have ONE customized insight ready (Section 3). Send a 3-5 question intake form (Typeform-style) so you walk in already knowing their stack and biggest manual process.

**Consent open (00:00–00:30):**
> "I use an AI notetaker to capture this accurately so I can send you a clean recap — any objection?"

(The visible notetaker bot doubles as recording notice — legally the safer default.)

**The call, mapped to the Challenger 6-step "Commercial Teaching" order — insight FIRST, your solution LAST ([salesmotion.io](https://www.salesmotion.io/blog/challenger-sales-methodologies)):**

| Min | Step | What you do |
|-----|------|-------------|
| 0:30–3:00 | **Warmer** — make it about THEM | "I had a look at how you run X and noticed a couple of things — mind if I walk you through what I found?" Show contextual knowledge of their industry. Do NOT pitch. The moment the "gift" smells like a veiled pitch, reciprocity dies ([insites.com](https://insites.com/how-to-use-free-audits-as-a-lead-generation-tool-for-your-marketing-agency)). |
| 3:00–7:00 | **Reframe** (the wow moment) + **Rational Drowning** | Deliver the ONE customized insight (Section 3). Lead with "What may surprise you is…" Back it with a number / their own data. |
| 7:00–9:00 | **Emotional Impact** | Quantify the hidden cost so the gap is *felt*, not just noted. "That's roughly $X/year leaking out." Pain is ~4x the motivator of gain ([dealhub.io — Gap Selling](https://dealhub.io/glossary/gap-selling/)). Optionally: a peer who ignored it and paid. |
| 9:00–12:00 | **A New Way** (solution-agnostic) | Describe the *capabilities* needed to fix it — NOT your product. "To fix this properly you'd need to map every step, score it, and sequence the build…" This is where you reveal there's a *bigger* problem under the one you just diagnosed. |
| 12:00–14:00 | **Your Solution** | "That's exactly what the full audit does." Now position the paid audit. The connection feels obvious. |
| 14:00–15:00 | **CTA with urgency** | Book the paid audit on the call. "I only take 2-3 of these a month — want me to hold a slot?" |

**Hard rules:**
- ✅ Cap free advice at ~5-10 min, then move them along ("Sell today, educate tomorrow"). A documented failure: a consultant spent 3 calls explaining the HOW and even mailed a book — prospect did it themselves and walked ([consultingsuccess.com](https://www.consultingsuccess.com/stop-giving-advice-away-for-free)).
- ✅ Give the **diagnosis** (what's wrong + why + that a gap exists). Hold the **prescription** (the implementation steps).
- ❌ Never email the audit. Present it live "like a proposal."
- **When they fish for the "how," redirect:** *"That's exactly the type of thing we'd build together in the paid audit."* ([sakasandcompany.com](https://sakasandcompany.com/sales-process-free-advice/))

## 3. The "One Mind-Blowing Insight" Tactic

This sits at the intersection of three robust mechanisms:
- **Reciprocity** — the gift creates obligation. It works **strongest** when meaningful, *customized*, and *unexpected*; a generic stat does far less (a plain mint lifted tips ~3.4%, but turning back to add a second "as a special exception" lifted them ~21% — the *manner* beat the *amount*) ([sybill.ai](https://www.sybill.ai/blogs/law-of-reciprocity-in-sales)). So **tailor the insight to THEIR situation** and frame it as "I don't usually do this on a first call."
- **Information-gap (Loewenstein)** — one good insight reveals a gap they didn't know existed AND that you see what they can't, opening a loop they feel compelled to close. (Works because a *partly-aware* prospect sits on the rising part of the curiosity curve — not a "more info = more curiosity" rule.)
- **Challenger reframe** — a counterintuitive insight proves competence. 74% of buyers choose the rep who *first* adds value (vendor-cited, treat magnitude as soft) ([sybill.ai](https://www.sybill.ai/blogs/law-of-reciprocity-in-sales)).

**The formula:** WHO (research the person) → WHAT (find the pattern they're not seeing) → HOW (deliver via intriguing framing, ideally citing a third-party number). Target reaction: *"Wow — I didn't know that, but I can see how it makes a huge difference."* ([naturaltraining.com](https://www.naturaltraining.com/blog/insight-selling-our-3-step-process/))

**Trigger phrases:** *"What may surprise you is…"* / *"For the longest time you've been told X, but we figured out Y."* Lead as a question when you can.

### 4 example insights a business owner would find jaw-dropping

> **1. The SaaS overlap leak.**
> *"What may surprise you — you're paying for at least three tools that do the same job. Most businesses run 20-40% software overlap or underuse. At your size that's roughly $[X]k/year you're burning before we automate a single thing."* (SaaS consolidation alone can hit payback in 30-90 days — [optimizewithsanwal.com](https://optimizewithsanwal.com/ai-automation-agency-pricing-2026-a-cfos-guide/).)

> **2. The hidden cost of one slow process.**
> *"You told me your team copies orders between [system A] and [system B] by hand — about 4 hours a week. That one task is costing you ~$[hours × loaded rate × 52]/year. And it's not the time, it's the typos hitting customers."* (Annual value = time/wk × loaded hourly cost × 52 — [vantagepoint.io](https://vantagepoint.io/blog/sf/automation-roi-how-to-pick-the-10-flows-that-actually-matter).)

> **3. The response-time reframe.**
> *"Your average lead response is ~12 hours. Cutting that to under 1 hour isn't a nicety — for a business your size it's the single biggest conversion lever you have, and it's automatable in a week."* (Dunn's framing: "reduce response time 68% = $161k/yr" — [newsletter.andrewdunn.co](https://newsletter.andrewdunn.co/p/how-to-run-an-ai-audit-that-actually-delivers-roi-not-just-hype).)

> **4. The "why AI projects fail" reframe (positions you as advisor, not vendor).**
> *"Here's the thing most agencies won't tell you: ~80% of AI projects fail — not because the tech is bad, but because companies skip strategy and jump straight to building. That's the real reason to scope before you build."* ([Liam Ottley, primary X thread](https://x.com/liamottley_/status/1978657166275105002))

Each insight names a real, quantified problem (the diagnosis) — and each implies a fix you don't hand over (the prescription).

## 4. Call Recording / Transcription Tool

**Recommendation: Fathom (free tier) as the capture + transcript layer → pipe the raw transcript into Claude/ChatGPT to manufacture the deliverable.**

**Why Fathom free:**
- **Unlimited recording, transcription (38 languages), and storage** on free — the only major tool with genuinely unlimited free recording ([help.fathom.video](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/5290881)). Competitors all cap the thing you actually need: Fireflies 800 min storage + ~20 AI credits/mo; Otter 300 min/mo; Read.ai 5 meetings/mo; tl;dv 10 AI notes *lifetime*.
- Highest G2 rating in category, ~30-sec post-call summaries, best-in-class action-item extraction ([get-alfred.ai](https://get-alfred.ai/blog/best-ai-meeting-notetakers)). (Note: the ~92-95% accuracy figure is vendor-claimed; real-world is closer to ~85-90% in mixed audio, up to ~95% clean.)
- **Treat it as a transcript engine, not a summarizer.** Free caps *in-app advanced summaries* at 5/mo, and AI follow-up emails / action items / "Ask Fathom" are Premium-only — but you don't care, because you feed the raw transcript to the LLM you already pay for and get a *custom audit template* the tool can't match.
- **Upgrade trigger (not default):** Fathom Premium (~$16/mo annual, $20/mo monthly) only once copy-pasting transcripts becomes a chore and you want one-click follow-ups + "Ask Fathom" Q&A across past calls.

**Hard-avoid Otter.ai for client/discovery calls** — active 2025-26 class-action privacy litigation (*In re Otter.AI Privacy Litigation*, N.D. Cal.); it records non-user participants by default on host-only consent ([natlawreview.com](https://natlawreview.com/article/take-note-new-wave-privacy-litigation-targets-ai-notetaker-otterai)). Reputational + legal risk when you don't control the room.

**Deliverable prompt (paste transcript below it):**
> Executive Summary (3-5 bullets) | Decisions Made (Decision | Rationale | Impact) | Action Items (Owner | Task | Deadline | Dependencies) | Risks & Open Questions | Next Steps. *Also identify implied action items where nothing was explicitly assigned.* ([brasstranscripts.com](https://brasstranscripts.com/blog/meeting-transcript-action-items-ai-prompt))

**Audit-grade add-on prompt:**
> *"Extract every decision discussed; for each, summarize the reasoning, trade-offs, and supporting arguments raised."*

**Ritual before sending:** fix speaker labels (2-3 min) → confirm every action item has an owner + deadline → ship within 24 hours.

## 5. The Paid Extensive Audit: Deliverable + Pricing

A productized, **fixed-fee** (never hourly), **2-3 week** engagement. Hourly signals you lack standardization; fixed-fee is the 2026 norm and aligns incentives ([ariesconsultinggroup.com](https://ariesconsultinggroup.com/blog/ai-readiness-audit-cost/)).

### The deliverable: a 15-25 page document, 4 named parts (a common pattern across vendors; counts vary) ([justinmckelvey.com](https://justinmckelvey.com/assessment))

- [ ] **1. Workflow Audit** — current-state map of how work actually flows
- [ ] **2. AI Opportunity Map** — 8-12 candidates ranked by impact/effort, plotted on a 2x2 (Quick Wins / Strategic Bets / Big Swings / Money Pits)
- [ ] **3. Tool Recommendations** — a vendor matrix mapping each opportunity to specific platforms, scoped to their stack + budget. Separate platform/consumption cost from your execution fee.
- [ ] **4. 30/60/90-Day Roadmap** — with owners, milestones, and effort estimates
- [ ] **+ Day-14 1-hour live walkthrough call** as the close. Bundle 30 days of post-audit support.

### How to score the opportunities

There's **no single industry-standard formula** — pick one and apply it consistently. Two solid options:

- **5-criteria scorecard (1-5 each, /25):** Business Impact, Feasibility, Data Readiness, Strategic Alignment, Speed to Value. Re-weight by context (weight Feasibility up if budget-constrained) ([fountaincity.tech](https://fountaincity.tech/resources/blog/a-strategic-framework-for-how-to-prioritize-ai-projects/)).
- **Weighted automation score** (one vendor's, not a standard): `(Time Saved ×3 × Volume ×2 × Frequency ×2) / (Effort ×1 + Risk ×1)` ([vantagepoint.io](https://vantagepoint.io/blog/sf/automation-roi-how-to-pick-the-10-flows-that-actually-matter)).

### Make ROI concrete (this is what justifies the build price)

`Net Annual Benefit = (Annual Hours Saved × Loaded Hourly Cost) + (Errors Prevented × Error Cost) − (Implementation + Maintenance)` ([latenode.com](https://latenode.com/blog/workflow-automation-business-processes/automation-roi-metrics/10-metrics-to-measure-automation-roi)). The 80% of ROI usually comes from just 10-15 flows; hunt the six high-yield SMB categories: data movement, notifications/reminders, document creation, scheduling, follow-up sequences, approval routing ([amazingbusinessresults.com](https://www.amazingbusinessresults.com/how-to-identify-automation-process-opportunities/)).

### Pricing (real numbers, 2026)

| Client size | Audit price | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| SMB (<$2M rev) | **$1,500–$8,000** | 1-2 wks |
| **Mid-market ($2M–$50M)** ← sweet spot | **$5,000–$15,000** | 2-3 wks |
| Enterprise ($100M+ / regulated) | $15,000–$50,000+ | 4-8 wks |

Sources: [ariesconsultinggroup.com](https://ariesconsultinggroup.com/blog/ai-readiness-audit-cost/) (one consultancy's model, consistent with vendor data points), corroborated by Ascend AI ($1,500 flat / 14 biz days), n8n-agency "AI Readiness Audit" ($5k-$15k / 2-4 wks).

**Beachhead pricing for a solo consultant just starting:** $1,500 to win the first one, then raise as you refine. Ottley's explicit advice: price the first audit low, increase over time ([scribd "$10,000 AI Audit" guide](https://www.scribd.com/document/890182441/How-to-Perform-Your-First-10-000-AI-Audit-as-an-AI-Agency)).

**Strategic decision — to credit the audit fee against the build or not:**
- **DON'T credit** if you want to be the *objective advisor* — crediting biases you toward recommending builds, and buyers now flag it as a red flag ([ariesconsultinggroup.com](https://ariesconsultinggroup.com/blog/ai-readiness-audit-cost/)).
- **DO credit** (charge $500-$2,000, credit it to the project) if your goal is conversion velocity over advisory positioning ([aibusiness.vc](https://aibusiness.vc/solo/how-to-charge-ai-automation-services)).
- **For a solo consultant starting out, lean "credit it"** — it removes friction on the build close. Switch to "don't credit" once your positioning is strong enough to charge for objectivity.

**Cap at 2-3 audits/month** to manufacture scarcity and protect quality.

## 6. The Audit → Retainer Close + Objection Handling

**The close (Andrew Dunn's method — don't quote the whole system upfront):**
1. From the audit, ask: *"What's the shortest, simplest path to value?"*
2. Scope the **smallest valuable piece** — a minimum-viable automation (~$1,200, delivered in 1 week).
3. **Ship the quick win → prove ROI with before/after data → THEN pitch the retainer:**
> *"I only work with clients on a retainer basis, because real impact comes from a long-term partnership, not a one-off build."* ([newsletter.andrewdunn.co](https://newsletter.andrewdunn.co/p/how-to-price-ai-implementations-after-an-audit-without-getting-burned))

**Anchor every price to the documented savings, not hours:**
> *"Your audit shows $100k/year going into this. We'll cut ~80% — $80k. Our fee is $20,000."* Price the build at **10-30% of documented annual savings** ([optimizewithsanwal.com](https://optimizewithsanwal.com/ai-automation-agency-pricing-2026-a-cfos-guide/); [aibusiness.vc](https://aibusiness.vc/solo/how-to-charge-ai-automation-services)).

**Present three tiers — Basic / Recommended / Premium.** ~68% of B2B buyers pick the middle when shown three; make the middle your target ([aibusiness.vc](https://aibusiness.vc/solo/how-to-charge-ai-automation-services)).

**Retainer structures:**
- **Support retainer:** $2,000-$3,000/mo, 5-10 hrs (maintenance, 1-3 automations)
- **Build retainer:** $10,000-$15,000/mo, 1-2 days/week, ~20% off hourly (keep building)

**Objection handling — the ADRB loop: Acknowledge → Diagnose → Reframe → Bridge** ([aimadefor.com](https://www.aimadefor.com/blog/ai-sales-objection-handling/)). Handle the real concern, not the words:

| They say | Real meaning | Your move |
|---|---|---|
| "Too expensive" | "I don't see enough value yet" | Quantify ROI on THEIR numbers + cost of inaction. "The audit showed this is costing $80k/year — the fee is a fraction of that." |
| "Need to think about it" | Not convinced | "What specific concern is still open?" |
| "Not the right time" | Not urgent enough | Compute the cost of waiting: "Every month you delay is ~$[X] gone." |
| "We'll do it in-house" | — | Reframe: custom builds make sense for proprietary workflows no SaaS covers — you give that capability without them maintaining a full dev team. |

**Closing checklist:**
- [ ] Live walkthrough done (not emailed)
- [ ] ROI quantified in their own dollars
- [ ] Smallest quick win scoped + priced separately
- [ ] Quick win shipped → before/after captured
- [ ] Three-tier proposal with a target middle tier
- [ ] Retainer framed as "how I work," not an upsell
- [ ] Build priced as 10-30% of documented savings
