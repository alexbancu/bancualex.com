# Anti-AI Slop Writing Guide

Readers detect AI writing instantly. Once they suspect it, you've lost them.

## Structural Red Flags to Avoid

### Binary Contrast Addiction
Don't write constant "it's not X, it's Y" oppositions:
- ❌ "success isn't about working hard, it's about working smart"
- ❌ "marketing isn't selling, it's storytelling"

**Fix:** Say what you mean directly with specific details.

### Triple Threat Syndrome
Don't group everything in threes:
- ❌ "fast, efficient, reliable"
- ❌ "powerful, simple, transformative"

**Fix:** Vary rhythm. Sometimes two things. Sometimes four. Sometimes one.

### Infomercial Transitions
Avoid these cringe questions:
- ❌ "the catch?"
- ❌ "want to know the secret?"
- ❌ "the brutal truth?"
- ❌ "here's the kicker"

**Fix:** If you wouldn't say it in conversation, don't write it.

### Corporate Verb Disease
Avoid stuffy -ing verbs:
- ❌ "highlighting the benefits"
- ❌ "emphasizing critical importance"
- ❌ "facilitating better outcomes"

**Fix:** Use simple, active verbs. "show" not "highlighting." "help" not "facilitating."

### Hedging Language
Don't clear your throat:
- ❌ "it's worth considering..."
- ❌ "you might want to think about..."
- ❌ "it's important to note that..."

**Fix:** State the opinion. Skip diplomatic warm-ups.

### Thesaurus Abuse
Use simple words:
- utilize → use
- execute → do
- facilitate → help
- implement → start
- optimize → improve
- leverage → use

## Formatting Giveaways

### Arrow Obsession
One → per post maximum. AI discovered this symbol and won't let go.

### Emoji Explosion
One emoji per section. Maybe. Or none. Don't treat emojis like confetti.

### Em Dash Overdose
Mix punctuation. Commas exist. Periods too. Don't chain 6 em dashes per paragraph.

## Phrase Patterns That Scream Robot

Avoid these specific patterns:

| Pattern | Example | Fix |
|---------|---------|-----|
| "No X. No Y. Just Z." / "Not X. Just Y." | "no fluff. no theory. just actionable insights." / "not a checklist to grind through. just the things that keep me moving." | Any other sentence structure |
| "Game-changer" variants | "the game has changed" / "supercharge your X" | Describe actual change without hyperbole |
| "Real" overload | "real strategy from real experts getting real results" | Show authenticity through specific examples |
| "If you're serious about..." | "if you're serious about growth, let's talk" | Vary CTAs, be specific about what happens next |
| "To your success" | Email sign-offs | "cheers," "talk soon," or just your name |
| "Not because of X. But because of Y." | "i didn't succeed because of luck. but because of hard work." | Don't package obvious things as insight |
| Short hook questions | "the best part?" / "want access?" / "ready to level up?" | Ask real questions requiring thought |
| "Enter: [Thing]" | "enter: my revolutionary framework" | Introduce things normally |
| "Stopped me in my tracks" | "my mentor said something that stopped me in my tracks" | Describe impact without melodrama |

## Content Red Flags

### Symbol Obsession
Don't explain what things "represent" or "symbolize."

**Fix:** Say what happened. Say what you learned. Skip literary analysis.

### Generic Case Studies
AI invents people when it lacks data. Usually named Sarah Chen.

**Fix:** Use actual examples. Real names (with permission). Real numbers.

### "Everything Changed"
Nothing "changes everything" or "transforms your entire business."

**Fix:** Describe specific changes with specific metrics.

## The Core Principle

Write like you talk. If a sentence could appear in anyone's content, delete it. If a phrase makes you cringe when read aloud, cut it.

Your voice is your moat. Don't fill it in with generic patterns.
